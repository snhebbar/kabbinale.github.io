The Natural Splendor of Kabbinale

Kabbinale is a unique settlement wrapped perfectly within wild mountain ranges and expansive evergreen woods. The pristine atmosphere is enriched by dense canopies, teeming wildlife habitats, and a diverse range of indigenous avian species.

The ecosystem serves as a vast natural repository of organic forest fruits and potent medicinal herbs, safeguarding age-old Ayurvedic botanical secrets passed down through generations.

Divine Temples & Daivasthanas

Explore the historic spiritual entities that protect and guide the village landscape.

Uma Maheshwara Vittalagundi

Situated in the scenic valley of Vittalagundi, this historic temple dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Uma stands as a major center of meditation, prayer, and community connection.

Lakshminarayana Melumata

Located on the elevated ridges of Melumata, this temple honors Lord Lakshminarayana. It preserves centuries-old traditional rituals and hosts grand community assemblies during seasonal festivals.

Venugopala Krishna Kelamata

The serene Kelamata sanctuary houses the divine shrine of Lord Venugopala Krishna, filling the atmosphere with peaceful devotional music and continuous spiritual energy.

Kodamanitthaya Daivasthana Melumata

An ancient shrine dedicated to the protective deity Kodamanitthaya on the Melumata ridge. It serves as a spiritual anchor for the village's historic lineage and seasonal cultural rituals.

Sathya Devathe Kelamata

A sacred space in Kelamata dedicated to the worship of Sathya Devathe, symbolizing truth and justice. The shrine plays an integral role in the cultural lifecycle of Kabbinale's families.

Kaalaraahvu Kshetra

The sacred grove dedicated to Kaalaraahvu, preserving the historical practices of nature-aligned worship within the dense forests of Kabbinale.
