Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Educated and cultured training is the ultimate remedy for every challenge in the world. We envision an equitable society where financial struggles never block a child's right to complete education and build a brighter future.

Our Mission

To bring together like-minded individuals who dedicate a portion of their monthly income, skills, and time to provide educational aid, honor national heroes, assist government schools, and encourage local talents.

About Unnathi Charitable Trust

Serving Society for 5 Continuous Years

Team Unnathi Charitable Trust (R.) Karkala was born out of a shared vision by a group of like-minded individuals. Members reserve a fraction of their monthly hard-earned income to uplift underprivileged students and support social welfare projects.

Help isn't restricted only to money. We believe collective small steps yield massive results. Anyone can contribute through active participation, time, guidance, or physical effort.

Events & Celebrations

Upcoming Special Event - August 15th

Independence Day & 5th Year Anniversary Celebration

Bailoor, Karkala Taluk

Every year on Independence Day, Team Unnathi celebrates in local government schools across Karkala. This 5th-year milestone event features:

  • Scholarship distribution of ₹5,000 each for 30 deserving students (Total ₹1,50,000).
  • Honoring 10 prominent local achievers from various fields.
  • Felicitation of SSLC & PUC distinction rank holders from Government Schools.
  • Honoring retired Army Soldiers & retired Police personnel for exemplary service.
